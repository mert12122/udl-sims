Directions

  1. Enter your data. Type each pressure and melting temperature from your journal table. Each point appears on the graph.
  2. Draw your curve. Click Draw Curve, then drag across the graph to draw a smooth curve through your points.
  3. Read the graph. Move the cursor over the graph to read the pressure and temperature at any point.
  4. Redraw if needed. Click Clear Drawing to erase your curve and try again.
